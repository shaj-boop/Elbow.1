# Safety — Elbow-1

v1 is a bench joint that may later go on one adult arm. It is not a medical device.

## Hard rules

1. **E-stop is armed or the motors do not enable.** Latching mushroom, wired so a software crash still kills drive.
2. **Torque cap in firmware** below stall. MX-28AT stall is 2.5 N·m at 12 V. v1 command limit is well under that.
3. **No backdrive fight.** If the load cell sees an unexpected spike, disable.
4. **Bench first.** 2 kg through 90° in a fixture, logged, before any worn test.
5. **Worn test is optional and slow.** Padded cuffs, second person present, e-stop in the free hand.
6. **No medical claims.** No rehab protocol. No patient. No "for disability" marketing in v1.

## What this is not

A full-body exo. A factory cobot. A product. A way around a clinic.
