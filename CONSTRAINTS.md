# Constraints — what v1 is not

- Not a suit. One elbow.
- Not two arms. Not a shoulder. Not a hand.
- Not Dynamixel-X series, not Maxon, not a custom cycloidal. MX-28AT ×2 is the joint.
- Not CNC aluminum cuffs in v1. PETG + webbing. If PETG cracks, that is data.
- Not ROS as a requirement. Arduino + a serial log on the Mac mini is enough.
- Not a paper. A video, a CSV of angle/load, and the CAD.

If a sentence starts with "later we could add hips," it does not belong in this grant.
