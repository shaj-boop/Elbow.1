# After the money lands

Clock time: about four to six weeks after the Dynamixels arrive.

1. **Day 0.** Order both MX-28ATs, the shield, the e-stop, the load cells. Print cuff v0 while they ship.
2. **Week 1 (bench, one motor).** Shield on the Uno. E-stop in the enable line. Spin one MX-28AT through a known angle. Log ticks. No cuff yet.
3. **Week 2.** Second actuator. Short 2020 frame. Load cell in the path. 2 kg mass, 90°, three cycles, CSV.
4. **Week 3.** PETG cuffs, webbing. Fit on the bench dummy (a padded tube), then on an arm with a second person and the mushroom in the free hand.
5. **Week 4.** Publish: STEP/STL, firmware, the CSV, a one-minute bench video, a one-minute worn video or an honest note that worn is not ready.
6. **If it fails.** The failure is the result. Still publish.

CAD lives on the existing Mac mini M4. That machine is not in the ask.
